## What they sell

- Press release distribution across Asia and worldwide, with translation into the languages above (site, 2026-10-22).
- A press release is written once and exported to several formats: NewsML-G2, NITF, email (MJML) and web HTML (Kris, Aug 2026). This is the core product the new CMS is built around.
- The site's keywords also name Reuters, Bloomberg, Thomson, Factiva, multimedia releases and IR services (site meta keywords, 2026-10-22). Which of these are real current distribution partners is not confirmed.

## Customers and market

- Companies and organizations that need to disclose and spread news to media and financial markets (site). Guess: mostly listed companies and IR teams in Asia, plus foreign companies reaching Asian media.
- The legacy database holds about 109,000 press releases in the `Article` table (Kris, Aug 2026).
